# Sample Risk Policy

## High-Risk Activity

Customer activity may be considered high risk when it includes unusual transaction volume, rapid movement of funds, activity inconsistent with stated business purpose, or transactions involving high-risk jurisdictions.

## Escalation Requirements

Cases involving suspicious patterns, unexplained high-value activity, or potential sanctions concerns should be escalated to a compliance manager for review.

## Analyst Responsibilities

Compliance analysts are responsible for reviewing available evidence, documenting rationale, and ensuring that final decisions are supported by applicable policy guidance.

## Human Review

AI-generated recommendations may be used as decision support, but final compliance decisions must be made by an authorized human reviewer.
